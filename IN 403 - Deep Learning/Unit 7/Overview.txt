AI Governance, Risk, and Compliance
In this unit, you will bring together everything you have practiced so far—model selection, evaluation, deployment guardrails, grounding, and monitoring—and apply it through an AI governance lens. You will examine how hospital AI systems can fail after deployment due to drift, false alarms, bias, privacy risks, or ungrounded responses, and you will practice making realistic decisions about whether a system should continue running, run with conditions, be paused, or be retired. Using a risk register, incident history, and a controls library, you will calculate risk scores, propose mitigations with owners and metrics, and write a model approval memo that reflects how organizations manage accountability and compliance for AI in production.
