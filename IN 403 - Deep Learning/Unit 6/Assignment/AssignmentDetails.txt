Detecting Data and Prediction Drift in Deployed AI Systems

Course Outcome practiced in this unit:
1. IN403-4: Analyze multiple case studies involving deep learning;
2. GEL-4.03: Understand patterns of human behavior based upon real world observation

In this assignment, you will analyze how deployed AI models degrade over time due to changes in data and behavior. Using a baseline dataset and a future dataset, you will detect data drift and prediction drift, interpret what the changes mean, and recommend actions a hospital should take when drift is identified. The focus is not on improving the model, but on monitoring, diagnosis, and responsible decision-making after deployment.

Files Provided
1. Baseline dataset (historical data), baseline_no_show.csv (https://kapextmediassl-a.akamaihd.net/IST/IN403/2602B/hospital_no_show_synthetic.csv or IN 403 - Deep Learning/Unit 6/Assignment/hospital_no_show_synthetic.csv)
2. Future dataset (new data collected after deployment), future_no_show.csv (https://kapextmediassl-a.akamaihd.net/IST/IN403/2602B/future_no_show.csv or IN 403 - Deep Learning/Unit 6/Assignment/future_no_show.csv)
3. IN403_Unit6_Drift_Detection_Lab_Template.ipynb (https://kapextmediassl-a.akamaihd.net/IST/IN403/2602B/IN403_Unit6_Drift_Lab.ipynb or IN 403 - Deep Learning/Unit 6/Assignment/IN403_Unit6_Drift_Lab.ipynb)

Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Open the provided notebook template in Google Colab.
2. Load the baseline dataset and the future dataset provided in the notebook.
3. Review the features used by the deployed model and confirm which variables are inputs and which represent predictions.
4. Data Drift Detection:
4.1. Compare feature distributions between the baseline and future datasets.
4.2. Use visualizations (such as histograms or density plots) and summary statistics to identify changes.
4.3. Identify at least one feature showing meaningful drift.
5. Prediction Drift Detection:
5.1. Compare model prediction distributions between the baseline and future datasets.
5.2. Visualize changes in prediction probabilities or class outputs.
5.3. Describe how the model’s behavior has shifted over time.
6. Drift Interpretation:
6.1. Explain what the observed drift suggests about changes in patient behavior, workflows, or data collection.
6.2. Discuss whether the drift represents a minor shift or a serious risk.
7. Response Recommendation:
7.1. Recommend one or more actions the hospital should take (for example: retraining, threshold adjustment, increased monitoring, or rollback).
7.2. Justify your recommendation based on patient safety and operational impact.
8. Answer the reflection questions in markdown at the end of the notebook.
8.1. Which features showed the most evidence of data drift, and why might those changes have occurred?
8.2. Did prediction drift occur even if some feature distributions appeared stable? Explain why this can happen.
8.3. What risks could arise if this drift went undetected in a hospital setting?
8.4. What action would you recommend first, and why?

Your notebook must include:
1. At least one visualization comparing baseline vs future feature distributions.
2. At least one visualization comparing baseline vs future prediction distributions.
3. A brief written interpretation of detected drift.
4. Reflection answers written in markdown.
