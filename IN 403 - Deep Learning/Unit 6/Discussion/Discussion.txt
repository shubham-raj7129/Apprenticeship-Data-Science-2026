Monitoring Drift in Deployed Hospital AI Systems

A hospital has deployed several AI systems to support operations and clinical decision-making, including a no-show prediction model, a PPE detection system, and a policy assistant for staff questions. All of these systems performed well during testing, but they have now been running in production for several months.

Recently, staff have reported that some predictions feel “off,” and leadership is concerned that changes in patient behavior, workflows, or data collection may be affecting model performance. The hospital must decide how to monitor these systems and what actions to take when performance degrades.
