MLOps & Drift Monitoring
In this unit, you will examine what happens to AI models after they are deployed in real-world environments. Even well-performing models can fail over time as data, user behavior, and operating conditions change. You will learn how to identify different types of drift, including data drift and prediction drift, and why they matter in high-risk settings such as healthcare. Building on the system-level thinking from earlier units, the focus is on monitoring, evaluation, and decision-making—recognizing when a model’s outputs can no longer be trusted and determining what actions should be taken to maintain safe and reliable performance.
