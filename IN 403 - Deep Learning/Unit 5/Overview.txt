Large Language Models, Embeddings, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
In this unit, you will move beyond training individual models and focus on designing modern AI systems that are safe, reliable, and usable in real organizational settings. You will build a hospital policy assistant that uses embeddings and vector search to retrieve relevant documents and ground large language model (LLM) responses in trusted sources. Through hands-on experimentation, you will examine why LLMs can hallucinate and how retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) reduces that risk. As in prior units, the emphasis is on applied decision-making: evaluating system behavior, citing sources, and recommending governance guardrails before deployment.
