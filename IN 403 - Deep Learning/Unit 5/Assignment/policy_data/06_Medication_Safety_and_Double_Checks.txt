Title: Medication Safety & Double Checks (Synthetic Policy)
Last Updated: 2025-08-12

High-Alert Medications
- Require an independent double check for dosing and patient identity.
- Examples: insulin infusions, anticoagulants, chemo agents.

Five Rights (Minimum Standard)
- Right patient, right medication, right dose, right route, right time.

Look-Alike / Sound-Alike
- Use barcode scanning whenever available.
- If barcode scanning fails, follow downtime procedure and document exception.

Reporting
- Near misses must be reported; focus is learning, not blame.
