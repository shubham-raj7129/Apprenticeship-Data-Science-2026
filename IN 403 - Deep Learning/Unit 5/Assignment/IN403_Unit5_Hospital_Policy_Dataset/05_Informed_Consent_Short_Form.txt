Title: Informed Consent – Short Form Guidance (Synthetic)
Last Updated: 2024-12-05

When Consent Is Required
- Consent is required before invasive procedures and for release of information beyond routine operations.

Consent Process (Short Form)
1) Explain procedure, benefits, risks, and alternatives in plain language.
2) Confirm patient understanding; allow questions.
3) Verify identity; ensure correct patient, procedure, and site.
4) Document consent in the EHR and obtain signature as required.

If Patient Lacks Capacity
- Follow surrogate decision-maker protocol and document rationale.

Note
- If there is a conflict between this short form and a unit-specific consent policy, escalate to the attending provider and Compliance.
