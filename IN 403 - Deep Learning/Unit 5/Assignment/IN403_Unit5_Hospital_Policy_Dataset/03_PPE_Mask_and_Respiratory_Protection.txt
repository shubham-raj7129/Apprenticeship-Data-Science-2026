Title: PPE – Mask & Respiratory Protection (Synthetic Policy)
Last Updated: 2025-10-10

General
- Masks are required in designated clinical zones as posted by signage.
- Staff must follow unit-specific requirements (e.g., ICU vs general clinic).

Mask Types
1) Surgical mask: standard clinical areas.
2) N95/respirator: required for aerosol-generating procedures or when indicated by isolation signage.
3) Eye protection: required when splashes/sprays are anticipated.

Patient/Visitor Guidance
- Provide a mask to patients/visitors upon request or when required by signage.
- If a patient cannot wear a mask due to medical reasons, document the exception and notify Charge Nurse.

If Someone Is Non-Compliant
- Use a supportive approach: offer a mask and explain the requirement.
- Do NOT physically restrain. If conflict escalates, call Security per unit protocol.
