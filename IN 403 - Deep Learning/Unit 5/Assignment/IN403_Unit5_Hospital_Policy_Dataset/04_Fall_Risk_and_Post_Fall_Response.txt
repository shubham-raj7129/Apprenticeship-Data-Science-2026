Title: Fall Risk & Post-Fall Response (Synthetic Policy)
Last Updated: 2025-07-20

Fall Risk Screening
- Screen all patients at admission and once per shift.
- High-risk indicators: prior falls, dizziness, sedating meds, mobility impairment.

If a Fall Occurs (Immediate Steps)
1) Stay with the patient; call for assistance.
2) Assess for injury (head/neck/back) before moving the patient.
3) If head injury is suspected, notify provider immediately and follow neuro-check protocol.
4) Document event in chart within 1 hour and submit incident report within 2 hours.

Environment Check
- Remove hazards (cords, spills), ensure call light accessible, confirm bed alarms if ordered.

Family Notification
- Notify family per care plan unless restricted for privacy reasons.
