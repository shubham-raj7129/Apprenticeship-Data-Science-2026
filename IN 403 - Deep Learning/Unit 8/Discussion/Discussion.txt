Defending an Enterprise AI System

A hospital network is preparing to launch a new enterprise AI system that combines multiple components: predictive models, a policy assistant powered by an LLM with retrieval, monitoring for drift, and a formal governance process. Before launch, leadership has asked for a final review to determine whether the system is ready for deployment and how it should be defended if questions arise from clinicians, compliance officers, or regulators.

Your role is to evaluate the system from an enterprise perspective and recommend whether it should move forward, under what conditions, and how it should be defended as a responsible AI deployment.

Initial Post:-

Describe an enterprise AI system similar to those studied in this course (for example: a hospital policy assistant, clinical risk prediction system, or operational monitoring tool). Then address the following:
1. System overview: Briefly describe the system’s purpose, users, and key components (models, data sources, monitoring, governance).
2. Deployment readiness: Explain whether you believe the system is ready for deployment today. Identify one strength that supports deployment and one risk that still needs to be managed.
3. Risk and safeguards: Describe two safeguards you would rely on to defend the system (for example: grounding with retrieval, human review, drift monitoring, access controls, or governance approvals).
4. Defense scenario: Explain how you would defend this system if challenged by a stakeholder who is concerned about safety, bias, privacy, or reliability.
End your post with a clear recommendation: deploy, deploy with conditions, or delay deployment.

3. Ask one question that would significantly change their deployment recommendation if answered differently
