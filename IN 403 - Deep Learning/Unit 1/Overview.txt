In this unit, you will focus on applied AI decision-making through a hands-on Google Colab lab using PyTorch. You will build and evaluate two predictive approaches for a hospital no-show scenario: a simple baseline model (logistic regression) and a small neural network. After comparing their performance and practical tradeoffs, you will recommend which model is the best fit for hospital operations and explain your reasoning for a non-technical audience. The goal is to practice making clear, evidence-based model choices that balance performance, interpretability, and operational needs.

After completing this unit, you should be able to:
Explain core deep learning algorithms, including their purposes, key components, and typical use cases.
Describe the structure and function of neural networks, including layers, activation functions, and how training occurs.

Course Outcomes practiced in this unit:
IN403-1: Create deep neural networks for application to the field of information technology;

GEL-1.02: Demonstrate college-level communication through composition of original materials in Standard English
