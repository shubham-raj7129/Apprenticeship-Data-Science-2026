Your Model Just Triggered Alerts — Do You Pause Deployment?

Scenario:
Your hospital deployed a no-show model and a policy RAG assistant. During the last two weeks, monitoring shows:
1. Increased drift and a drop in performance for the no-show model
2. Decreased retrieval hit rate and increased hallucinations for the RAG assistant.
3. User complaints increased during peak clinical hours

Leadership asks: "Should we continue, continue with conditions, or pause/rollback?"

Initial Post Instructions (required):
1. Pick ONE system (No-show OR RAG) and describe what metrics you would watch daily.
2. Define the 3 alert thresholds that matter most and explain why.
3. Recommend a response: continue / continue with conditions / pause / rollback.
4. Describe a containment action (safe-mode or human-in-the-loop) you would use immediately.
5. List who must be notified (roles) and what the escalation path is.

Peer Responses:
Reply to 2 of your peers. For each reply:
1. Challenge their thresholds with a stronger safety argument.
2. Suggest one additional diagnostic check (data quality, labeling delay, segment drift).
3. Add one prevention step (tests, monitoring automation, change management).
