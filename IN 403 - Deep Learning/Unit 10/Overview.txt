Production Monitoring and Incident Response
In Unit 10, you moved from building AI systems to operating them safely in production. You learned how to monitor deployed models using telemetry and user feedback, detect drift and quality degradation, and translate metrics into actionable alert thresholds. You also practiced incident response by creating a mini runbook that outlines how to contain risk, diagnose root causes, remediate issues, and verify recovery before resuming normal operations which focuses on reinforcing evidence-based decisions to continue, restrict, or roll back deployment when risk increases.
