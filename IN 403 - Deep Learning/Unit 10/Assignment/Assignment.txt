Production Monitoring and Incident Response for Deployed AI Systems

Course Outcome practiced in this unit:
IN403-6: Examine risks and controls in deployed AI systems.

In this assignment, you will take on the role of an AI operations and reliability analyst for a hospital. You will analyze production monitoring signals, detect potential model failures, and design an incident response plan for a deployed AI system. Unlike earlier assignments that focused on model design, governance, or security, this assignment focuses on operational decision-making focusing on determining when a system is functioning normally versus when it poses risk and requires intervention.

The goal is to move from monitoring signals to structured incident response and evidence-based deployment decisions.

Step-by-Step Instructions:-
1. Review the Production Scenario
1.1. In the notebook, review the deployed hospital AI system scenario and the provided monitoring signals.
1.2. Identify the system being monitored (e.g., prediction model, vision system, or LLM assistant) and its operational role in the hospital.
2. Analyze Monitoring Signals. Using the provided data in the notebook, analyze at least three types of monitoring signals. Examples may include:
2.1. Model performance metrics (accuracy, error rate, precision/recall)
2.2. Data or prediction drift indicators
2.3. Usage patterns or anomaly rates
2.4. User feedback or incident reports
    Describe what each signal suggests about system health.
3. Identify Potential Incidents. Determine whether the monitoring signals indicate:
3.1. Normal variation
3.2. Degradation in performance
3.3. Elevated operational risk
3.4. A potential production incident
    Explain your reasoning using evidence from the metrics and trends.
4. Incident Severity Assessment. Classify the situation using an incident severity level (for example: low, medium, high, or critical). For your chosen severity level, explain:
4.1. Potential impact on patient safety or operations
4.2. Likelihood of harm if no action is taken
4.3. Urgency of response
5. Design an Incident Response Plan. Create a structured response plan that includes:
5.1. Immediate actions (e.g., restrict system use, adjust thresholds, notify teams)
5.2. Technical remediation steps (e.g., retraining, rollback, configuration changes)
5.3. Escalation workflow (who is notified and when)
5.4. Verification steps to confirm the system is safe to resume normal operation
6. Operational Decision. Make a clear operational recommendation:
6.1. Continue deployment
6.2. Restrict deployment
6.3. Roll back or pause the system
    Justify your decision based on monitoring evidence and risk assessment.
7. Production Operations Memo. Write a 350–500 word Production Operations Memo in markdown addressing the following:
7.1. System overview and operational context
7.2. Key monitoring signals and what they indicate
7.3. Incident severity and risk rationale
7.4. Response actions and escalation plan
7.5. Operational decision (continue, restrict, or roll back)
7.6. Ongoing monitoring and alert thresholds
