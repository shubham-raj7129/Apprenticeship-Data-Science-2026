Computer Vision Systems (Detection, Classification, and Deployment Risk)
In this unit, you will work with practical computer vision systems commonly used in healthcare and other operational settings. Using PyTorch, you will build an image classification model with transfer learning and examine how vision models are trained with limited data. You will also learn to distinguish between image classification, object detection, and identity recognition, and why those differences matter for real-world deployment. As in prior units, the emphasis is on applied decision-making: building and evaluating a working model, understanding error types, and recommending responsible guardrails that address privacy, bias, and operational risk before deployment.
