LLM Security, Red Teaming, and Guardrails
In Unit 9, you will focus on what can go wrong when LLM-powered systems are deployed in real hospital environments—and how to reduce that risk without shutting the system down. You will examine common attack and misuse patterns such as prompt injection, attempts to extract PHI or internal documents, jailbreaks, and requests for unsafe medical advice. Building on earlier units (RAG grounding, drift monitoring, and governance), the emphasis shifts to designing and testing a practical guardrail layer that can decide when to allow a request, refuse it, or escalate to a human. You will then evaluate your guardrails using a red-team prompt dataset and make a deployment recommendation based on measured safety performance.
