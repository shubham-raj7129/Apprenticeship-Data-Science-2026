Purpose
This assignment requires you to compose a report covering human expression on the workplace as affected by machine learning now and in the future. The target audience for the report should be for senior-level executives who need an understanding of how machine learning can affect the workplace. It also fulfills the university-mandated communication requirement for the course.

Assignment Instructions
Based on research and the knowledge you have achieved thus far in this class, compose an informative report covering human expression on the workplace as affected by machine learning. Your paper should follow APA guidelines and be at least 3 pages in length, not including the required title and reference pages. Research and provide details covering current affects machine learning has on the workplace. Also, cover five positive and five negative affects machine learning may have on human expression now. Use examples to make your points. After that, explore the efficiency, and profitability, automated machine learning processes may have in the workplace. Include how this affects human expression, employment, and work possibilities. Conclude with your opinion on the status of future affects machine learning will have on human expressions in the workplace. Be sure to support your position and use examples as appropriate. Your report must include title and reference pages. You must have at least three references. Make sure to follow the most current APA guidelines.

Report Parameters/Expectations:
    Include a title page and reference page.
    Written work is provided in Standard English.
    At least 3 pages in length, not counting the title page and reference page.
    Includes a highly developed viewpoint, purpose, and exceptional written content.
