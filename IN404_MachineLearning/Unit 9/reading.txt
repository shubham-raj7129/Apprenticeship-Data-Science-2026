    Chapter 8: Decision Trees and Ensemble Learning

Reading Summary:
In this unit, you will read about various methods for making predictions. Some of the models covered are decision trees, K-means clustering, and Naïve Bayes. For each, you will read about what they are and when to use them for various data sets. You will read about feature extraction and principal component analysis to reduce your analysis to the necessary columns. Finally, you will read more about using scikit-learn with decision trees.
