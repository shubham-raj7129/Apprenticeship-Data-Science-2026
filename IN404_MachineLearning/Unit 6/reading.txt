Reading Summary:
In this unit, you will read about the matplotlib architecture to understand the backend, artist, and scripting layers. You will also read about matplotlib application programming interfaces (APIs) covering pyplot. You may note that pyplot has been used quite a bit for visualizations. Finally, you will read about various visualizations such as scatter and bubble plots, pie charts, histograms, and heat maps.
