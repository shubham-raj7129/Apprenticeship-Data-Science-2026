Kernel Analysis
In this unit, you will learn about kernel analysis by exploring the results of the support vector model to include a confusion matrix and classification report. You will also explore the results and look at time-series analysis in visualizations using line and bar charts. You will learn about the matplotlib architecture to understand the various layers. You will also learn about matplotlib application programming interfaces (APIs) covering pylab and pyplot. Finally, you will use the results of your Python program, to get a heat map, decision boundary, and scatter plot.
