This week, I want you to focus on one important idea: How does a machine learning model understand text?

When we read a review, message, or comment, we understand the words and their meaning. A computer, however, needs text to be converted into numbers before a machine learning model can work with it.

One common method is called Count Vectorization (Bag of Words).

For example:

Sentence 1: I love this product

Sentence 2: I hate this product

Count Vectorization identifies the words and counts how often they appear. This turns the sentences into numbers that a machine learning model can use.

Why is this important?
Once text is converted into numbers, a model such as logistic regression can use those numbers to identify patterns and make predictions, such as:

Positive vs. negative reviews

Spam vs. non-spam messages

Sentiment in social media posts

A good question to ask yourself is:

What is the model learning from the text?
